Airbase — The AI Operating System for the Trades

A calm control room for a company that runs itself.

AI agents book, dispatch, invoice, and follow up. The operator supervises — approving the few decisions that need a second set of eyes and handling the exceptions that are genuinely human. Most screens are monitoring surfaces, not to-do lists. This document defines the visual language and the screens to build against.

Foundations

Color

Warm neutrals carry the entire interface; there is no competing brand accent. Identity and primary actions are ink. Semantic colors are low-chroma so nothing shouts. The AI accent is the one deliberately cool note — it always means "machine-proposed, not yet human-confirmed."

The cool periwinkle never decorates. If something is periwinkle, the AI proposed it and a human has not yet confirmed it. The moment a human confirms, it turns warm — ink text on paper, or the green "confirmed" pill. This single rule is the backbone of the whole product.

Foundations

Typography

Hanken Grotesk is the single workhorse — dense data tables and natural-language AI output share one humanist grotesk. Numbers are always tabular. No display serif: this is an operations tool, not a memorial.

Scale — Hanken Grotesk
Display / 30 · 600
Page titles, big KPI numbers
Heading / 22 · 600
Section & card titles
Body / 15 · 400 — the resting size for forms, lists, and reading inside any workspace.
Small / 13 · 400 — captions, helper text, metadata
Label / 11 · 600 · uppercase

AI-generated copy lives on the cool tinted surface with the ✦ glyph; human-entered copy is ink on paper. Same typeface, different ground.

Space & Elevation

A 4px base grid. Breathing room is the default in the operator app; the dispatch board earns density because it is a monitoring surface. Elevation is soft and warm-tinted — shadows suggest paper, not glass.

Components

Controls

Quiet by default. The primary action is the only filled ink button on a screen — green is never an action fill, only a confirmed state. Hit targets never below 40px; 44px on touch, 56px in the field app.

Buttons
Confirming an AI proposal uses the ink primary too — the result becomes a green "Confirmed" pill (see AI Confidence Pattern). The button itself is never green.

Status & Provenance

Every job, every AI decision, and every field carries a calm, legible state. Operational status uses a pill with a single colored dot. AI decisions carry their own lifecycle. Provenance answers one question at a glance: did a human or a machine put this here?

Components · the trust pattern

AI Confidence Pattern

The most important component in the product. Every AI decision shows its confidence, its reasoning in plain English, and a link back to where it came from. Below threshold it waits for a human; above, it acts and tells you what it did. Confirming turns it warm — the visual language of trust earned.
